# ADR 0003 — Rights-aware editorial media
- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-24

## Decision
Every article has attributed cover media including source URL, creator/owner, descriptive alt, license and photo/illustration classification. Initial covers are generated in this project as original editorial vector illustrations. Journalistic photographs from commercial agencies will not be copied without permission or an explicit reuse license.

## Consequences
The first edition is visually complete while avoiding infringement. Future editors may commit properly licensed photos, or embed remote authorized media, with full attribution and a rights review.
