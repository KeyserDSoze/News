# ADR 0003 — Rights-aware editorial media
- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-24

## Decision
Every article has attributed cover media including source URL, creator/owner, descriptive alt, license and photo/illustration classification. Most initial covers are generated in this project as original editorial vector illustrations. The typhoon story uses a licensed JMA/NASA satellite image dated 20 October 2026 and the olive-harvest story a clearly marked 2008 archival photo by Orrling under CC BY-SA 3.0; both cite the Commons licensing page and fall back to original vector artwork if the remote image cannot be served. Journalistic photographs from commercial agencies will not be copied without permission or an explicit reuse license.

## Consequences
The first edition is visually complete while avoiding infringement. Future editors may commit properly licensed photos, or embed remote authorized media, with full attribution and a rights review.
